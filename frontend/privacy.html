Privacy Policy

Jai Maa Bhadrakali Studio respects your privacy. This page explains how information is handled when you contact us or submit an enquiry.

Information we receive

When you submit the date enquiry form, we may receive your name, phone number, event details, date, location, selected service, package preference and message.

How we use it

We use these details only to check availability, prepare a quotation, reply to your enquiry and coordinate the requested photography, video or live-streaming service.

Sharing and retention

We do not sell enquiry information. Details may be shared with a relevant studio team member only when needed to deliver your requested service. We retain information only as long as needed for communication and business records.
